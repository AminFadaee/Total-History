## Changed

- **The deck is called Total History.** The deck, its note types and the file (`total_history.apkg`) take the new name.
- **Legacy lines stand on their own.** A figure's legacy line no longer leans on the sentence before it ("Three months later …", "In this position …"), and only the person's own name is hidden: a line that names someone else inside their full name (Augustus for Tiberius), gives a nickname or another name, or names something after them (the Fibonacci numbers) is passed over for the next best sentence.
- **Figures are filed under the right state more often.** A generic title no longer makes someone a state's ruler (every Sultan of Delhi had been filed under the Beylik of Karaman), and citizenship no longer files French writers under Algeria or Austrians under each crown land.

## Fixed

- A full review of every civilization and figure corrected wrong states (enemies and places of death), successions, modern countries, map years, roles, life dates, Known for lines and legacy lines, each correction citing its source.
- Where sources disagree, the card says so: the Sunda Kingdom began in 669 or 932.
