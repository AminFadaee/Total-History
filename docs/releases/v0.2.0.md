## Added

- **Conflicts.** A new note type for the wars, battles, revolts and conquests that matter most to the deck's civilizations: about 430 conflict notes, drawn from five sets per civilization (the big world wars it fought, the conflict that founded it, its defining one, the one that ended it, and a second major war), chosen by rules from Wikidata and Wikipedia and corrected by a reviewed list in which every entry cites the page it was checked against.
  - **Who fought:** "Who fought: Battle of Carrhae" → "Roman Republic against Parthian Empire".
  - **Which conflict:** a map of the battlefield with the two sides painted and named, and the year → "Battle of Carrhae".
  - **When:** for the big wars and the best-known conflicts only (Wikipedia's Vital Articles levels 3 and 4), and only when Wikidata and Wikipedia agree on the years; single battles are not asked.
  - The back says what the conflict was, its result, its sides and what it was to each civilization ("Ended Aztec Triple Alliance"), and notes when sources differ on its years.
- **Rise and Fall.** Two new cards on the civilization note ask how a civilization began and how it ended: "How it began: Republic of Texas" → "Texas Revolution (1835–1836)"; "How it ended: Aztec Triple Alliance" → "Spanish conquest of the Aztec Empire (1519–1521)". About 100 Rise and 200 Fall cards.
- Conflicts are tagged `TH::Category::Conflicts`, with the civilizations they belong to, their centuries and the highest tier among their civilizations, and come in the study order after the civilization and its people. In all the conflicts add about 800 cards; the Essential tier is now about 600.

## Upgrading

- Import as usual. The civilization note type gains the Rise and Fall cards and fields, so Anki may ask for a one-way sync after the import. Existing cards and their reviews are kept: the new card types come after the old ones.
